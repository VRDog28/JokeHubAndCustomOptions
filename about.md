## The mod

Joke & customization hub

You can use it if you want to do a challenge, or if you want to do a prank.
You can use it if you want your icon to be drawn by me :D
Or you want a new death sound

## Features

- Change icon to one of these ( dog.png, cat.png, cube.png all self drawn btw :) ) 
- Change death sound to one of these ( kaboom.ogg, vine_boom.ogg, musical.ogg musical_2.ogg )

## Jokes 
- Blackes out part when you die (configurable)
- Reduces your fps cap everytime you die (configurable)
- Reduces gravity everytime you die (configurable)
- Make the play button run away from your cursor

More features and jokes coming soon!
